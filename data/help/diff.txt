= Diff Viewer | Diff

The diff viewer shows side‑by‑side differences between two files.

Keys (typical):
  - Up/Down, PageUp/PageDown: navigate
  - n / N: next/previous hunk
  - F5: merge current hunk (when supported)
  - F10: quit
