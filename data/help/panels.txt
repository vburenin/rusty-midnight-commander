= Panels

Rusty Midnight Commander shows two directory panels side by side.

Basics:
  - The highlighted row is the cursor; the highlighted panel is active.
  - F9 opens the top menu bar. F10 quits.
  - Use Up/Down, PageUp/PageDown, Home/End to move.
  - Enter opens a directory; Backspace goes to parent; Tab switches panel.
  - F3 views the selected file in the internal viewer.
