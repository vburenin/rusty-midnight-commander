# Apache-2.0 original help text
= Virtual file system | VFS

Panels can browse more than a local directory. Enter on a supported
archive (tar, tar.gz/tgz, zip, cpio, ar, rpm, deb, iso, rar, 7z, lha/lzh)
opens it as a directory. `..` leaves the archive and returns to the
parent path. Zoo archives are not browsable in this build.

extfs helpers can list and copy out of other archive-like trees. Copy
from an archive into a local panel extracts. Remote links (FTP/SFTP and
similar) are configured from the Left/Right menus when those backends
are available.

Command-line manuals (original Apache-2.0 text, not GNU GPL man pages):
`man mcr`, `man mcr-edit`, `man mcr-view`, `man mcr-diff`. From a source
tree: `man -l docs/man/mcr.1`.
